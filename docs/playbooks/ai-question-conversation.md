# Talk Through an AI Question

## Goal

Help someone frame an AI-related question clearly enough to make a better next decision. This conversation is exploratory; it does not promise a complete implementation plan.

## Before the conversation

1. Review the context the person chose to share.
2. Write down the likely decision behind the stated question.
3. Note technical, operational, and human considerations worth testing.
4. Prepare no more material than the conversation reasonably needs.

## Suggested 45-minute shape

- **5 minutes — Context:** What is happening, and why does it matter now?
- **10 minutes — Desired outcome:** What would be different if this went well?
- **15 minutes — System:** Examine workflow, data, users, constraints, and existing attempts.
- **10 minutes — Judgment:** Identify risks, tradeoffs, and decisions that should remain human-owned.
- **5 minutes — Next step:** Agree on the smallest useful action or experiment.

Use the shape flexibly. The purpose is clarity, not completing an agenda.

## Boundaries

- Distinguish technical possibility from verified feasibility.
- Say when legal, medical, financial, security, or domain expertise is needed.
- Do not ask for credentials, confidential datasets, or unnecessary personal information.
- Do not imply that an exploratory conversation includes implementation support.

## After the conversation

1. Send the promised next step or concise recap when appropriate.
2. Update the request status.
3. Invite feedback only after delivering value.
4. Complete a private copy of the [conversation debrief](../templates/conversation-debrief.md) within 24 hours.
5. Use the [conversation-learning playbook](conversation-learning.md) to record consistent codes and aggregate themes without identifying the participant.
6. If the question requires a structured organizational plan, offer the Implementation Roadmap Deep Dive without pressure.
