# Lead response

## Goal

Respond promptly, protect private context, and give each legitimate request a clear next step.

## Intake channels

- Website contact form
- Calendly booking
- Published business phone by call or text
- Direct referral or introduction

The private dashboard is the source of truth for website contact requests. Calendly, phone, and referral activity remain in their originating systems unless a follow-up needs to be represented in the dashboard.

## Triage checklist

1. Confirm who is reaching out and what outcome they appear to want.
2. Decide whether the request is a thoughtful AI conversation, a business implementation question, a collaboration, a media/writing inquiry, or unrelated.
3. Do not copy sensitive personal or organizational details into GitHub.
4. Reply with one clear next step: answer directly, request essential context, offer a booking link, or decline.
5. Update the dashboard status to `contacted`, `follow_up`, `closed`, or `ignore`.

## Response target

- Review new requests on business days.
- Aim to send a first response within two business days.
- If a complete answer will take longer, acknowledge the request and state when to expect the follow-up.

This is an operating target, not a public service-level promise.

## Qualification prompts

Ask only what is necessary:

- What decision or outcome are you working toward?
- Who will use or be affected by the result?
- What have you already tried?
- Is there a deadline or constraint that changes the recommendation?

## Closing the loop

A request is complete when the next step has occurred, been explicitly declined, or no response is needed. Record reusable, anonymized lessons in an issue or writing draft; never paste the private conversation.
