# Implementation Roadmap Deep Dive

## Goal

Turn one concrete organizational AI opportunity into a decision-ready starting roadmap. The session should narrow the problem and define a credible first move, not pretend to replace discovery or delivery work.

Use the copy-ready [`implementation-roadmap.md`](../templates/implementation-roadmap.md) for the client deliverable. The fictional [`example-implementation-roadmap.md`](../templates/example-implementation-roadmap.md) shows the intended level of specificity without using customer information.

## Required intake

Collect only what is needed to prepare:

- desired business outcome;
- current workflow and its owner;
- intended users or affected teams;
- available data and systems, described without sharing secrets;
- known constraints, deadline, and decision-maker; and
- what has already been attempted.

If the opportunity cannot be discussed safely without confidential material, agree on a sanitized description before the session.

## Suggested 30-minute shape

- **5 minutes — Outcome and scope**
- **10 minutes — Current workflow, people, and data**
- **10 minutes — Pilot, dependencies, and risks**
- **5 minutes — Decision, owner, and next checkpoint**

## Minimum deliverable

Provide a concise written roadmap containing:

1. Opportunity and desired outcome
2. Current-state workflow
3. Users, stakeholders, and decision owner
4. Proposed smallest useful pilot
5. Data and system dependencies
6. Human-review and escalation points
7. Risks and open questions
8. Success measures
9. Named next action, owner, and target date

## Delivery workflow

1. Duplicate the template into a private, client-appropriate workspace.
2. Complete only the context that is known before the call; mark assumptions instead of presenting them as facts.
3. Use the session to resolve the decision, pilot boundary, human-review points, and next owner.
4. Send the concise roadmap after correcting factual misunderstandings.
5. Keep research, implementation, vendor selection, and ongoing advising outside the deliverable unless separately scoped.

The fictional example was used as a simulated session to test the template end to end. That test led to explicit sections for the decision gate, out-of-scope work, baselines and targets, human escalation, and the next owner/date.

## Quality bar

The roadmap is useful when someone can decide whether to proceed and knows what evidence the first pilot must produce. Avoid broad tool lists, generic automation advice, and timelines unsupported by the available information.

## Follow-up

- Correct factual misunderstandings promptly.
- Treat additional research, implementation, or ongoing advising as separately scoped work.
- Keep the customer's roadmap private unless explicit publication permission is obtained.
- Request feedback on clarity, confidence, and usefulness; testimonial permission remains separate.
