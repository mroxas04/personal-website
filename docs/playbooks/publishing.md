# Publishing

## Goal

Publish accurate, useful work while protecting private information and keeping the site's promises consistent.

## Applies to

- Papers, articles, and blog posts
- Portfolio projects and images
- Testimonials and quotations
- Offer, pricing, duration, booking, and payment changes
- Metadata, structured data, `llms.txt`, and social previews

## Pre-publication checklist

1. Confirm the intended audience and the one idea or action the page should support.
2. Verify names, dates, roles, links, prices, and status claims.
3. Remove secrets, private messages, customer data, unpublished client details, and unnecessary personal identifiers.
4. Confirm explicit permission for named people, organizations, photographs, and quotations.
5. For testimonials, verify whether consent is private-only, anonymous quotation, or named quotation.
6. Add descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
7. Keep canonical metadata, structured data, sitemap behavior, and `llms.txt` consistent with the public page.
8. Update `content/writing.ts` when a writing item's status or canonical URL changes.
9. Run the repository's validation workflow and verify the affected route after deployment.

## Material business changes

Changes to positioning, audience, pricing, payment identifiers, or the distinction among exploratory conversation, coaching, and consulting require an entry in the decision log in `OPERATING.md`.

## Corrections

Correct material errors transparently and promptly. If a publication exposed private information, use the incident-response playbook before making further public statements.
